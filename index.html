SCENE 01 · THE PERSON

Hi, I’m Anushka 👋

Engineer by training.Designer by practice.

Mechanical engineering taught me to think in systems, constraints and cause-and-effect. Product design taught me to point that thinking at people: how they use things, where things fail them, and what they quietly work around.

I’m interested in the problems that don’t fit neatly into one discipline, where the hardware, the software and the human have to agree with each other.

SCENE 04 · THE METHOD

i don’t start with the interface.
i start with the system.

01 — UNDERSTAND

See the system behind the problem.

People · behaviour · data · constraints

02 — DEFINE

Find where reality breaks the model.

Friction · gaps · dependencies · opportunities

03 — MAKE

Turn an insight into something testable.

Sketch · prototype · interface · product

04 — VALIDATE

Put it back into the real world.

Test · measure · learn · iterate
